Design details

The production logo is a simplified vector refinement of a concept created with the built-in image-generation tool. Native VectorDrawables provide sharp in-app branding, adaptive launcher icons, and monochrome themed icons.

Primary ink-on-lime actions have an 11.7:1 contrast ratio. Main body text exceeds 13:1 in the light theme. Hold annotation colors retain their existing meaning.
